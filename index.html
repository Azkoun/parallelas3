Process

From brief to shipped page.

The same four moves, whether the deliverable is a landing page, a dashboard, or this section right here.

Analyze

Pull product type, audience, style keywords, and target stack out of the brief.

Reason

Weigh 192 industry profiles to pick a pattern, palette, and typography pairing that actually fit.

Compose

Assemble a token-based system: color, type, spacing, motion, and effects, defined once and reused everywhere.

Ship

Deliver accessible, responsive, production-ready markup — no placeholder Lorem Ipsum required.
