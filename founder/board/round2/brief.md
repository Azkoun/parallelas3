# Board brief, round 2: four candidates designed to clear 8/10

## The founder
Lebanese, early-stage. Wants a high-ticket AI business sold to companies, with investor appeal and the ability to scale far
beyond Lebanon. Unfair advantage: Lebanese network (in Lebanon and the diaspora in the Gulf, West Africa, Europe and LatAm),
languages (Lebanese Arabic, Gulf-intelligible Arabic, French, English), understanding of how Lebanese-run businesses operate.
Budget, team and technical skill are NOT given (open questions). Do not invent them.

## What round 1 taught (the bar)
Round 1 tested an AI chief of staff for absent owners (avg 5.0, read as a consultancy), AI collections (6.0, crowded,
no moat) and WhatsApp AI ordering for distributors (6.7, competitors Choco, BeatRoute, Sukhiba and Cravin exist). The board
said an 8+ needs: (1) a buyer losing large, measurable money, sold on a revenue or recovered-cash outcome, ideally priced on
success; (2) a moat that compounds: proprietary data, network effects, or rules knowledge per counterparty;
(3) a market not already owned by funded players in the target region; (4) a one-sentence product used often;
(5) a real reason the Lebanese founder wins distribution.

## Candidate D: "ClaimBack" (AI claims-denial recovery for Gulf hospitals and clinics)
- What it is: AI that checks every insurance claim before submission against each insurer's rules (coding, documentation,
  pre-authorisation), predicts denials and fixes them, then auto-drafts appeals for already-denied claims. Arabic and English.
- Who: private hospitals, polyclinics and clinic groups in Saudi Arabia, UAE and Qatar (mandatory health insurance markets),
  then Lebanon, Egypt and Jordan. Buyer: CFO / revenue-cycle head / owner.
- Price: setup $10k-$40k + % of recovered and protected revenue (e.g. 5-15% of denied money recovered) or a monthly fee per facility.
- Lebanese edge: Lebanese doctors, hospital administrators and medical-billing staff are widespread in Gulf healthcare;
  Lebanese hospital groups and medical networks give warm introductions.

## Candidate E: "BidDesk" (AI tender and bid engine for contractors and engineering firms)
- What it is: AI that reads a tender package (often thousands of pages of specs, drawings lists, BOQs, contract terms),
  produces the compliance matrix, flags risky clauses, drafts technical proposals in the firm's style from past bids, and
  helps price the BOQ from the firm's own historical rates, cutting bid preparation from weeks to days.
- Who: mid-to-large contractors, MEP subcontractors and engineering consultancies bidding on Gulf megaprojects
  (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar), many of them Lebanese-owned or Lebanese-staffed.
- Price: $5k-$25k/month per firm + optional success fee on won contracts.
- Lebanese edge: Lebanese engineers, estimators and contractors are deeply embedded in Gulf construction and consultancy.

## Candidate F: "Order Desk Network" (refined winner of round 1)
- What it is: a WhatsApp number retailers (pharmacies, groceries) use to order from ALL their suppliers by voice note in
  dialect. Free for retailers; distributors pay per order or monthly; later embedded trade credit (pay-later for shops),
  financed against the order and payment history only the network has.
- Who: one vertical, one city first (pharma or FMCG, Beirut / Mount Lebanon), then Lebanese-owned distributors in West Africa and the Gulf.
- Price: distributors $1k-$5k/month or 0.5-1.5% of order value; credit earns a fee per financed order.

## Candidate G: "TradeDesk" (AI customs, duty and trade-document engine for importers)
- What it is: AI that classifies products to HS codes, prepares customs declarations and trade documents, checks free-trade
  agreement eligibility and catches overpaid duties (with refund claims), for importers and customs brokers.
- Who: importers, distributors and trading houses in Lebanon, the Gulf and Lebanese-owned trading houses in West Africa;
  customs brokers as a channel.
- Price: per-shipment fee + % of duty savings recovered; brokers on a monthly licence.
- Lebanese edge: Lebanese families run trading and import houses across West Africa, the Gulf and LatAm.

## What the board is asked to do
Score EACH of D, E, F, G through your lens (1-10, strict: an 8 must be earned). For each, also say exactly what change to
the idea would raise its score by 2 points. Then name the one you would fund.
