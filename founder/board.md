## 2. Risks raised by more than one member

1. **The product is not a moat on its own** (Offers on C, Monopoly on B and C). Dialect WhatsApp AI will become a commodity. Funded players already exist: Choco, BeatRoute (Africa), Sukhiba (Kenya) and Cravin (MENA) in ordering, and ClearGrid, InDebted and Ebra in collections. The edge has to be Lebanon plus the Lebanese-owned trading houses abroad, and network effects on the retailer side.
2. **Salesmen and relationships push back** (Monopoly, Product, and Offers on B). Salesmen hold the retailer relationship and often collect cash by hand. Both C and B threaten them.
3. **A is a consultancy in disguise** (all three). It is high-ticket, but bespoke deployments do not compound. Keep its best parts (leak detection, the owner's daily voice brief) as a premium add-on, not as the company.
4. **Accuracy and dirty data** (Product, Monopoly). Wrong orders, stale stock and price lists, and dollar-cash reconciliation.

## 3. Conditions checklist (to be tested by the rest of the pack)

- [ ] Pick ONE vertical and ONE city (e.g. pharma or FMCG distributors, Beirut and Mount Lebanon). → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] Measure what share of a pilot distributor's orders already arrive over WhatsApp, voice or phone. → real interviews / `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] Retailers prefer the agent to their salesman or a call; test the retailer side, not only the owner. → `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] Order accuracy target with human review below a confidence threshold, so no wrong order ever ships. → `/founder-ops`
- [ ] Sell on revenue, not cost savings (Offers lens condition): show more orders captured, faster reorders and fewer stock-outs, with a guarantee. → `/founder-offer`
- [ ] Prove the network effect: retailers order through the agent from at least 2 distributors. → `/founder-launch` pilot
- [ ] Salesmen become account managers, with a dashboard and commission kept, so they don't sabotage it. → `/founder-ops`
- [ ] Unit economics: setup plus monthly fee vs. the cost of AI, WhatsApp API and human review per order. → `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] Sizing: how many distributors in the chosen vertical, in Lebanon and in the Lebanese diaspora (Gulf, West Africa). → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] Open founder questions: budget, team (technical co-founder?), runway.

## 4. The strongest version the board can see

It is not exactly what was pitched. The winner is **C as the entry point, with B and A stacked on top**. Start by becoming the one WhatsApp number that pharmacies or grocers in one Lebanese city use to order from all their suppliers by voice note in dialect. The order is confirmed in under 30 seconds and lands straight in the distributor's ERP. Distributors pay setup plus monthly, and the pitch is "more orders, faster reorders, no missed orders", not "fewer salesmen". The order and payment history this builds is data nobody else has. From it come the premium layers: payment reminders and later trade credit (B, which the data makes defensible), and an owner dashboard with a daily voice brief and leak alerts (A). Then follow Lebanese-owned distributors into West Africa and the Gulf. The Offers lens's dissent stands: unless the offer is tied to measurable revenue and a guarantee, C risks being a commodity bot. `/founder-offer` must fix that.
