# Working name: TBD (idea tournament, three candidates)

The founder asked the board to pick the best of three candidates, not to test one idea.

- Founder: Lebanese, early-stage, wants a high-ticket AI product sold to business owners
  (Lebanese owners in Lebanon and in the diaspora: Gulf, West Africa, Europe, Latin America),
  that investors would fund, that can scale beyond Lebanon, and that could make the founder a millionaire.
- Budget and constraints: budget NOT given (open question). Team, technical skills and runway NOT given (open questions).
  Founder's unfair advantage: Lebanese network, language (Lebanese Arabic, French, English), understanding of how
  Lebanese family businesses actually run.

## Candidate A: "Owner's Eye" (AI Chief of Staff for absent owners)
- What it is: an AI layer that reads a company's WhatsApp groups, voice notes, POS, Excel and cash logs, sends the
  owner a daily voice brief in Lebanese dialect, and flags money leaking out (stock vs sales gaps, odd supplier prices,
  cash gaps, ghost discounts). Later: a "Founder Twin" that captures the owner's decision logic for succession.
- Who it is for: owners of Lebanese family businesses with 30-500 staff, often running entities in 2-3 countries,
  frequently not physically present.
- Price: setup $25k-$100k (4-8 weeks of on-site deployment) + $3k-$15k/month retainer; optional success fee on recovered leakage.
- How: sold through referrals and personal networks; delivered by forward-deployed engineers.

## Candidate B: "Collect" (AI receivables / collections agent)
- What it is: an AI agent that chases unpaid invoices for B2B distributors and wholesalers over WhatsApp, voice calls
  and voice notes in local dialects (Lebanese, Gulf, French for West Africa), negotiates payment plans within rules the
  owner sets, logs promises to pay, escalates to humans, and reconciles payments.
- Who it is for: distributors, importers, wholesalers and contractors that sell on credit (30-120 day terms) to many
  small retailers; Lebanon first, then Gulf and Lebanese-owned trading houses in West Africa.
- Price: $2k-$5k/month platform fee + 1-3% success fee on collected overdue receivables; setup $5k-$15k.
- How: sold to owners/CFOs through networks and chambers of commerce; integrates with their accounting/ERP or Excel.

## Candidate C: "Order Desk" (AI WhatsApp order-taking for distributors)
- What it is: an AI agent that takes orders from retailers (shops, pharmacies, restaurants) by WhatsApp text and voice
  notes in dialect, checks stock and price lists, confirms, upsells, and pushes the order into the distributor's ERP,
  replacing salesmen/call-centre order-taking and handwritten orders.
- Who it is for: FMCG, pharma and food distributors with hundreds to thousands of retail clients in Lebanon, Gulf, West Africa.
- Price: setup $10k-$30k + $2k-$8k/month, or a % of order volume processed.
- How: same channels as B; pilots with 1-2 distributors, then case studies.
